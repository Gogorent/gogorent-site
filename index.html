What drivers say

Before you book

Can I rent a car without a deposit?

Yes, on selected cars. The exact terms are confirmed before you book.

Is full insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is in the daily price. We explain the exact cover for your car before pickup.

Can I get the car at Tbilisi Airport?

Yes. Airport delivery and return are arranged in WhatsApp, as is delivery to your hotel or another agreed place.

Which car should I choose for mountain roads?

For Kazbegi, Gudauri and winter travel, choose an AWD or 4x4 SUV: the Mazda CX-9, the Mazda CX-5 or the Jeep Renegade 4x4.

What can I add to the rental?

A private driver from $80 a day, unlimited Wi-Fi from $5 a day, a baby seat from $5 a day, and a roof box or ski rack on request.

Do you offer long-term rental?

Yes. Long-term prices are given on request and depend on the car, the season and the rental period.
